ABOUT MOTORVAULT

A decade of trust. Hand-picked vehicles. Service that lasts beyond the sale.

Our Story

From 10 cars to Pakistan's most trusted vault.

MotorVault began in 2014 in a modest 10-car showroom on Murree Road, Islamabad — founded by two engineers who were tired of how opaque, transactional, and stressful buying a used car had become.

A decade later, that same showroom has grown into a 500+ vehicle inventory spread across multiple facilities, with a 200-point inspection process that has become the industry benchmark. We've placed cars with more than 2,000 families across Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, and Karachi.

What hasn't changed: every car is inspected by the same team, every price is fixed, and every owner gets a number they can actually call after the keys change hands.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Four promises that define every transaction.

Verified Vehicles

A 200-point mechanical, electrical, and documentation inspection on every car before it gets listed. If we wouldn't buy it, we won't sell it.

Best Price Guarantee

We benchmark every listing against current market data. Find the same car cheaper at a verified dealer in Pakistan and we beat the price — no haggling, no theatrics.

Easy Financing

Direct relationships with HBL, UBL, Meezan, and Bank Alfalah let us turn around financing approvals in 48 hours — often less, with rates we negotiate on your behalf.

After-Sale Support

Every car ships with a 3-month mechanical warranty and a dedicated service contact. The deal closes when you say it does — not the day you sign.
